# Basic Autocorrect Instagram launch

## Caption

My phone can fix a missing apostrophe. Meanwhile, I’m in Google Docs staring at an underlined “cant.”

You know what I meant. Just add the damn apostrophe.

So I built Basic Autocorrect. It fixes obvious typos automatically as you type, including the ones Google Docs misses.

cant → can’t. Typo fixed. Underline gone. Keep writing.

Free. Runs locally. No AI. Your keystrokes stay in your browser.

Available for Chrome and Firefox. Both links in my bio.
